# HPC Website — mid-fidelity wireframe

Next.js (App Router) build of the new High Performance Coatings site. This is
a **mid-fidelity wireframe**, not the final production build: real layout,
real navigation, real photos, and draft copy — not final brand polish, and
not yet the final CMS decision (see the WordPress-vs-Next.js comparison in
the proposal doc under `HPC Sitemap`).

## Status

- **Global nav** (`src/components/Header.jsx`, data in `src/data/nav.js`) --
  **service-led**: Services (Floor Coatings, Commercial Painting, Industrial
  Coatings, Log Home & Wood Restoration, Specialty), Industries (Education,
  Government & Public Safety, Retail & Hospitality, Automotive & Car Wash,
  Industrial & Manufacturing, Residential & Lake Homes), Gallery, Resources,
  About, Contact. This replaced the original audience-led IA
  (Residential / Commercial & Industrial) after the Facebook audit (370
  posts, 2020-2026) showed the work has moved toward commercial,
  institutional and industrial jobs. Old `/residential/*` and
  `/commercial/*` paths permanently redirect (`next.config.mjs`).
- **Home page** -- breadth-first hero, "Trusted by" client logo marquee
  (`src/components/LogoMarquee.jsx`, logos in `public/logos/clients/`,
  list + sources in `clientLogos` in `src/data/industries.js`), 5 service
  lines, industries grid, proof stats, industrial spotlight, recent work.
- **Service pages** -- `/floor-coatings` (+ `/residential`, `/commercial`),
  `/commercial-painting`, `/industrial-coatings`, `/log-home-restoration`,
  `/specialty` (+ `/murals`). Hubs at `/services` and `/industries`.
- **Industry pages** -- `/industries/[slug]`, generated from
  `src/data/industries.js`.
- **Our Work** (`/projects`, old `/gallery` redirects) -- project cards
  filtered by service line and industry, plus a "From the field" grid of
  one-off photos with a lightbox.
- **Project pages** (`/projects/[slug]`, 87 of them) -- case-study layout:
  hero, fact strip, short write-up + scope of work, then the photos as a
  story (Before -> The work -> The finished result), service process link,
  related projects, CTA. Write-ups live in `src/data/projects.js` (keyed by
  gallery album); they describe only what the photos show -- add
  locations, dates, products and client quotes as they come in.

Client/project names come from HPC's public Facebook posts and photo
signage. Open questions and the logo collection list are in
`docs/client-logos-todo.md` (non-blocking). A few service cards still show
"Photos coming soon" where no photo exists yet.

## Content & data

- `src/data/nav.js` -- nav structure; `src/data/industries.js` -- service
  lines, industry pages and client names.
- **Gallery** -- `src/data/gallery.json` (624 photos in ~140 job albums) is
  generated by `scripts/build-gallery.py` from `scripts/gallery-curation.json`.
  The curation file has one hand-reviewed record for **every** source photo
  (873: HPC's 237 Facebook photos, the 2017 Drive export, the old-site
  scrape, and 363 from sister company J&C Coatings' Facebook -- the raw J&C
  folder `jc-coatings-facebook-photos/` sits in the repo root, gitignored): keep/reject + reason, caption, service line, industry, finish,
  before/in-progress/after, quality, and an album `group` key. To retitle,
  retag or drop a photo, edit that file and re-run the script; album names
  and album-level industry fixes live at the top of the script.
- Rejected on review: stock photography and manufacturer swatch/catalog
  images from the old Wix site (only the 3 old-site photos carrying HPC's own
  logo were kept), text banners, people-posing shots, duplicates, and photos
  with no visible work.
- Pages reference photos by id via `photo()` / `photosFor()` in
  `src/lib/gallery.js`, so a dropped photo fails the build instead of
  silently lingering.
- `public/gallery/work/` -- resized (max 1400px, q78, EXIF-rotated) outputs.
  `public/gallery/murals/` -- Lake Byllesby case-study images + video.

## Spray-paint effect

`src/lib/spray.js` is a pointillist spray engine: a single nozzle makes one
right-to-left pass, round solid dots land around it in a gaussian cloud
(loose overspray ahead of the front, dense behind it), and the fill builds by
more dots landing -- nothing grows and nothing is faded in afterward (the
dots overlap ~8x, so coverage is ~99.9% as the nozzle passes). Behavior is modeled on the "Spray Dots Effect" project's
`SprayFillCanvas`. `SprayFillBand` (scroll-triggered stripes / full-section
fills) and `SprayHoverFill` (reversible button hover) draw with it. An
earlier port of RocoFoam's spray (circles growing until they merge) read as
foam, so it was dropped.
